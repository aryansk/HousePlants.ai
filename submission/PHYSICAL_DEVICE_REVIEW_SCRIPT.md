# HousePlants.ai — Physical Device Review Script

This is the evidence Apple explicitly requested for the Guideline 5.2.5 WeatherKit attribution
issue. It must be captured on a real iPhone running the latest installed iOS version, not in
Simulator.

## Before recording

- Connect and unlock the physical iPhone.
- Install the new replacement build containing the WeatherKit attribution fix.
- Start from a clean app state if possible.
- Enable screen recording and ensure the recording begins before launching HousePlants.ai.
- Record the exact iPhone model and exact iOS version shown in Settings → General → About.
- If demonstrating Plant Identifier, configure only a reviewer-safe Pl@ntNet key and use a non-sensitive plant photo. Otherwise state that this optional service is not enabled because the user supplies the key.

## Recording sequence

1. Start recording on the Home Screen.
2. Launch HousePlants.ai.
3. Tap **Enter your jungle**.
4. Browse **Discover**, search or open a plant, and show its care guide.
5. Add the plant to **My Jungle**.
6. Show a care action such as watering, journaling, or a health update.
7. Long-press the plant card, choose **Insights**, and show the **Weather-aware watering** section.
8. Show the visible ` Weather` mark and openable legal attribution link. If the official
   WeatherKit mark is loaded, keep it in frame; otherwise show the visible fallback and explain
   that the link opens Apple’s WeatherKit legal attribution page.
9. If available on the device, demonstrate the optional location flow used by WeatherKit.
   Explain that the core app remains usable if location permission is unavailable.
10. Open **Tools** and show the care calculators and seasonal care tools.
11. If enabled, open **Plant Identifier**, show the relevant photo/camera permission prompt,
   choose a plant photo, and show the result or the clear “API key required” state.
12. Open **Profile** and show the privacy information and the **Delete All My Data** control.
   Do not confirm the irreversible deletion during the recording unless the test data is disposable.
13. Stop recording after the main flow is complete.
