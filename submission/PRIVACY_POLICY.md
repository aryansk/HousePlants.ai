# HousePlants.ai Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 14, 2026**

HousePlants.ai is a local-first plant-care app. We do not operate user accounts or sell personal information.

## Information used by the app

- Information you enter in the app, including a username, city, plant collection, watering history, journal entries, care preferences, and optional photos, is stored on your device.
- If you choose Plant Identifier, the selected plant photo is sent over HTTPS to Pl@ntNet so it can return identification results. You provide and control the Pl@ntNet API key used for that request.
- If you grant Location permission and use climate-aware care, the app uses your approximate location to request a forecast from Apple WeatherKit.

## How information is used

The app uses this information to provide plant-care reminders, local recommendations, health calculations, journal features, plant identification, and climate-aware watering adjustments. When iCloud is available, selected plant state and preferences may be mirrored through Apple's private iCloud key-value store. Journal and profile photos remain on the device.

The app does not use analytics or advertising tracking, and we do not sell personal information.

## Third-party services

Pl@ntNet, Apple WeatherKit, iCloud, HomeKit, Calendar, and Notifications may process information when you use the corresponding feature or grant the corresponding permission. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Photos submitted to Pl@ntNet are handled under Pl@ntNet's own terms. HousePlants.ai has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Your choices

You can edit or remove profile information in the app, disable permissions in iOS Settings, and use **Profile → Delete All My Data** to remove data stored by the app. Deleting app data is irreversible. You can also remove your Pl@ntNet key from the Plant Identifier key sheet.
